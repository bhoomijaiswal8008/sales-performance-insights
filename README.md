# 📈 Sales Performance Insights: Tracking the Money Trail! 💰

Welcome! This project is a deep dive into E-commerce sales data. I analyzed customer behavior, product popularity, and delivery timelines to understand what drives a business forward. 

From **Valentine's Day** surges to **Diwali** rushes, I’ve mapped it all out! 🎁✨

### 🧐 What’s the Story?
I worked with a multi-table dataset (Customers, Products, and Orders) to uncover:
- **Revenue Analytics:** Hit a total revenue of **₹415,192** with deep dives into monthly growth.
- **Top Performers:** Identified that **'Colors'** and **'Soft Toys'** are the absolute fan favorites. 🧸🎨
- **Efficiency Tracking:** Calculated an average delivery time of **5.4 days**—helping the business identify bottlenecks.
- **Geographic Reach:** Analyzed orders across cities like **Varanasi, Kanpur, and Bhopal** to see where the brand is most loved.

### 🛠️ Prerequisites & Setup
1. **Microsoft Excel:** Best viewed in Excel to see the VLOOKUPs and Pivot magic in action.
2. **Analytical Mindset:** Ready to see how data turns into business strategy!

### ⚙️ Technical Skills Showcased
- **Data Modeling:** Linking multiple tables (Orders + Products + Customers) using VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP.
- **Time-Series Analysis:** Tracking sales across different months and days of the week.
- **Dashboarding:** Using Slicers for 'Occasions' (Birthday, Anniversary, etc.) to see seasonal impacts.
